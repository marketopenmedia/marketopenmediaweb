Privacy Policy

Market Open Media · Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Market Open Media ("Market Open Media," "we," "us," or "our") provides local marketing services, including Google Map Pack SEO, to local home service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website (the "Site"), respond to our ads, contact us, or become a client.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us:

  • Name, business name, email address, phone number, and business address
  • Information submitted through website forms, Facebook/Instagram lead forms, email, phone calls, or text messages
  • Business details needed to deliver services, such as your service area, target keywords, Google Business Profile link, and competitors
  • Billing information (payments are processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers)

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring URLs
  • Information collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies (see Section 4)

Information from third parties:

  • Lead information from advertising platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) when you submit a lead form
  • Publicly available business information, such as Google Maps listings and reviews

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Provide, manage, and improve our services
  • Send proposals, agreements, invoices, and service updates
  • Send marketing communications (you can opt out at any time)
  • Measure and improve our advertising and website performance
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

3. SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, we may send messages about your inquiry, appointments, and services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

4. Cookies, Pixels, and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our Site and to measure and deliver relevant advertising. These tools may collect information about your activity on our Site and other websites.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage ad preferences through Meta Ad Preferences and opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., CRM, email, scheduling, payment processing, hosting), who are required to protect it
  • Advertising and analytics partners, such as Meta and Google, as described above
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Client Confidentiality

Information about our clients' businesses is treated as confidential and used only to deliver our services, consistent with our Service Agreement.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described here, including legal, accounting, and reporting requirements.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing, including targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us using the information below. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
