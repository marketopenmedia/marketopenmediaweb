How Does Google Local Services Ads Ranking Work?

Market Open Media · Updated September 24, 2026

LSA ranking isn't just "whoever pays the most." Here's what Google says actually determines your position.

Google ranks Local Services Ads on three factors: your bid, the likelihood your ad will generate a lead, and your profile quality. Bid alone doesn't win — a contractor with a lower bid but a stronger, faster-responding profile can outrank one who bids higher but responds slowly.

1. Your Bid

Google's own description: your bid is the maximum amount you're willing to pay for a lead, though you may end up paying less. This sets a ceiling, not a guarantee of position.

2. Likelihood of Generating a Lead

This factor considers your responsiveness to inquiries (missed calls count against you), the search context (what service was searched, when, and where), how relevant your business is to that specific search, and whether you have messaging or booking options available.

3. Profile Quality

This includes your star rating, number of reviews, average response time, image quality, and the verification checks you've completed. Source: Google Local Services Help — "About ad rankings".

Why Response Time Matters So Much

Responsiveness shows up in two of the three official factors — it's part of "likelihood of generating a lead" and it factors into profile quality. Missed calls specifically count against you in Google's own ranking logic. This is exactly why missed call tracking is part of how we manage LSA accounts.

What This Means in Practice

Raising your bid without fixing response time or profile quality is throwing money at one-third of the equation. The fastest, most controllable win for most contractors is answering every call and keeping the profile complete — not simply outbidding the competition. This is what we manage as part of LSA management.
