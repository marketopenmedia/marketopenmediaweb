What Are Google Local Services Ads?

Google Local Services Ads are the "Google Guaranteed" listings that appear at the very top of Google — above regular search results and regular Google Ads — when someone searches for a local service. Unlike traditional pay-per-click advertising, LSA is pay-per-lead: you're only charged when a real, qualified lead contacts you.

How Google LSA Management Works

We set up and manage your Google Local Services Ads profile, including the Google Guarantee badge, so you appear at the very top of search results. You pay only for qualified leads, and we optimize spend and lead quality on an ongoing basis.

Google LSA vs. Google Maps SEO

LSA puts you at the very top of Google immediately, and you pay only for qualified leads. Google Maps SEO builds long-term organic visibility in local search and the Maps 3-pack at no cost per lead. Most contractors get the best results running both together — LSA for immediate leads while Maps SEO compounds in the background.
