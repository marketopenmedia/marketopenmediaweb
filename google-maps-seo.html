What is Google Maps SEO?

Google Maps SEO is the process of optimizing a local business's Google Business Profile (GBP) and website so it ranks higher in the Google Maps 3-pack and local search results — driving more calls, direction requests, and booked jobs from nearby customers actively searching for that service.

Unlike paid ads, Google Maps SEO is organic: you don't pay per click or per lead to appear in the Map Pack. Rankings are earned through relevance, proximity, and how well your Google Business Profile and website signal that you're the right business for the search.

Who Google Maps SEO Works For

We work with local service businesses that depend on phone calls and booked jobs — plumbers, electricians, HVAC companies, roofers, tree service companies, and similar contractors, anywhere in the United States. If customers find you by searching "[your service] near me," Google Maps SEO is built for your business.

Google Maps SEO vs. Google LSA

Google Maps SEO builds long-term organic visibility in local search and the Maps 3-pack at no cost per lead. Google Local Services Ads (LSA) puts you at the very top of Google immediately, and you pay only for qualified leads. Most contractors get the best results running both together — LSA for immediate leads while Maps SEO compounds in the background.
